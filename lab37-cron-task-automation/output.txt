Task 2.2 - Schedule One-Time Tasks with 'at'
------------------------------------------------------------
$ echo "echo 'Hello from at command!' >> /home/centos/at_test.txt" | at now + 2 minutes
warning: commands will be executed using /bin/sh
job 1 at Mon Aug 18 12:33:00 2026


$ echo "echo 'Scheduled task executed at $(date)' >> /home/centos/scheduled_task.txt" | at 16:30
warning: commands will be executed using /bin/sh
job 2 at Mon Aug 18 16:30:00 2026


$ echo "echo 'Tomorrow task: $(date)' >> /home/centos/tomorrow_task.txt" | at 10:00 tomorrow
warning: commands will be executed using /bin/sh
job 3 at Tue Aug 19 10:00:00 2026


$ atq

$ at -c 1 | head -15
#!/bin/sh
# atrun uid=1000 gid=1000

PATH=/usr/local/bin:/usr/bin:/bin
