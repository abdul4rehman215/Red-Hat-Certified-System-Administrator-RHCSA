-------------------------------------------------
Simulate CPU Load
-------------------------------------------------
$ stress --cpu 1 &
[1] 1523

$ pgrep -l stress
1523 stress

-------------------------------------------------
Graceful Termination
-------------------------------------------------
$ kill 1523

$ ps -p 1523
(no output)

Process terminated successfully.

-------------------------------------------------
Forceful Termination
-------------------------------------------------
$ stress --cpu 1 &
[1] 1602

$ kill -9 1602

Process immediately terminated.

-------------------------------------------------
Using killall
-------------------------------------------------
$ stress --cpu 1 &
$ stress --cpu 1 &

$ killall stress

$ pgrep stress
(no output)

All stress processes terminated.

-------------------------------------------------
Launch with nice
-------------------------------------------------
$ nice -n 19 yes > /dev/null &
[1] 1701

$ top

PID   USER   PR  NI  %CPU COMMAND
1701  toor   39  19  98.5 yes

-------------------------------------------------
Modify Priority with renice
-------------------------------------------------
$ sudo renice -n 10 -p 1701
1701 (process ID) old priority 19, new priority 10

$ top

PID   USER   PR  NI  %CPU COMMAND
1701  toor   30  10  97.8 yes

-------------------------------------------------
Cleanup
-------------------------------------------------
$ killall yes
